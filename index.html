Finance types

Conventional and
Murabaha finance,
both supported.

Switch your project to Murabaha and every label across the entire platform changes — "Interest" becomes "Profit share", "Lender" becomes "Finance Provider", "Maturity date" becomes "Settlement date". No configuration needed.

STANDARD
Conventional Finance
Standard UK development finance. Interest accrues on drawn balance. Retained interest deducted at each tranche release.
ISLAMIC FINANCE
Murabaha
Sharia-compliant development finance. Total profit agreed upfront. No daily interest accrual. Fully Sharia-compliant tracking.
